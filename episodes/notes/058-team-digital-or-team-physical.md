# 058 · Team Digital or Team Physical?

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ynBcgPh8xV4) · [Transcript](../transcripts/058-team-digital-or-team-physical.md)

## In one sentence
A personal aside: Sakurai buys games digitally because he values the play experience over packaging and space is precious, and the one thing he wants most from digital libraries is a way to sort purchases into folders.

## Key ideas
- **Physical:** you can keep it forever, it doesn't eat disk space, limited editions are collectible, and you don't need a point card or credit card to buy one. **Digital:** you can buy it the second you want it, it never goes out of stock, there's no store trip or wait for delivery, and it needs no shelf space.
- Sakurai is "Team Digital all the way." People sometimes give him physical copies, but when he buys for himself he buys digitally. He doesn't get attached to physical things and wouldn't call himself a collector.
- With his old company he had dedicated storage space; since going independent, finding room has been a struggle. He keeps his discs in a binder with synthetic sleeves, because he cares about the experience of playing, not the packaging.
- Digital console games are still pricey: the player supplies the storage and there's no packaging, yet the price is about the same as physical. There are reasons for that, possibly market issues, which he doesn't go into.
- Players are lucky to have the choice. As someone who plays a lot of games, what he always wants most is a way to sort his purchases into folders.
- He buys books digitally too, more than 6,000 e-books, and wonders how much shelf space they would need. Space is vital.

## Do this
- If you build a game library, launcher or storefront, let players sort their purchases into folders; it's what heavy players like Sakurai want most.
