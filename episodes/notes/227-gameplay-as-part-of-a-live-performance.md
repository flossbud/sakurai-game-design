# 227 · Gameplay as Part of a Live Performance

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ofubdMhvBM) · [Transcript](../transcripts/227-gameplay-as-part-of-a-live-performance.md)

## In one sentence
Live, one-time stakes, such as a real chance of failing in front of an audience, are what make a gameplay performance thrilling and memorable.

## Key ideas
- At the PRESS START 2012 concert, Kid Icarus: Uprising was on the program. Sakurai played the Chapter 12 Air Battle, "Wrath of the Reset Bomb", live with the orchestra. Uprising's Air Battles last five minutes, and the original piece is by Mr. Yuzo Koshiro.
- He made it hard on purpose: Intensity at the maximum of 9.0, a weapon with average attack power, and zero defense.
- The stage has a mid-boss, so the clear time depended on how fast he beat it, and the orchestra had to match its performance to when he did.
- The failure risk was real. A Game Over would stop the performance with no restart, and the audience would miss out. He believes that made it a real thrill, and the reactions and feedback said exactly that.
- The heart of PRESS START was that everything happened live, only for the people who were there. The performances came and went, and that is a large part of what made them special.
- The rest of the video recreates the playthrough over the orchestra recording. It isn't footage from the event, and the audio hasn't been mastered.

## Do this
- When you show a game live, keep real stakes (high difficulty, no restarts) so the audience feels a genuine chance of failure.
- If live or adaptive music must follow gameplay of varying length (e.g., a mid-boss whose kill time varies), plan the cue points where the music waits for or follows the player's actual progress.
- For a performance, pick a segment of known length (Uprising's Air Battles run about five minutes) so the music and gameplay can be planned together.

## Examples from the episode
- **Kid Icarus: Uprising — Chapter 12 Air Battle at PRESS START 2012:** played live at Intensity 9.0 with zero defense, with the orchestra following the timing of the mid-boss kill. A Game Over would have ended the performance.
