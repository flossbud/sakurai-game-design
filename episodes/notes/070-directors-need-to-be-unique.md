# 070 · Directors Need to Be Unique

**Category:** Work Ethic · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXBS7hk0B7k) · [Transcript](../transcripts/070-directors-need-to-be-unique.md)

## In one sentence
To be a director, develop a keen, personal way of seeing details and perspectives others miss, and pair it with good balance and clear communication, because that unique touch is what sets a game apart.

## Key ideas
- The question he's asked most is "How exactly do I become a director?" The skilled directors he knows don't have much in common. Each works completely differently.
- If they share one thing, it is a keen sensibility for seeing things differently from other people. Talking with them, he hears insights and perspectives he had never considered.
- Show them something or pitch an idea, and they latch onto tiny details and notice what most people miss, in visuals, game essence, animation and story. They even perceive people differently. That makes directors a little quirky, and he thinks that's a good thing.
- Quirkiness isn't enough: directors also need a good sense of balance and the skill to communicate their creative ideas. He can hardly think of a director who is bad at talking with people.
- Directors take their own thoughts and feelings and use them to set their games apart. Like a game, the role calls for a personal touch only that person can offer.

## Do this
- Train your eye: when you review a build, a video or another studio's game, write down the small details most people would miss (in visuals, game essence, animation and story) and what you would change.
- For each major design decision, ask what *your* personal take is and how it differs from the genre's usual answer. Use those differences on purpose to set the game apart.
- Practice explaining your creative ideas to teammates until they can repeat them back. A unique vision only helps if you can communicate it.

## Avoid
- Assuming there is one standard way to direct, and copying it.
- Being so idiosyncratic that you lose balance or can't communicate your ideas to the team.

## Examples from the episode
- None from specific games. He speaks generally about the many directors he has met.

## Quote
> A director position—just like a game itself— calls for a personal, unique touch that only that person is capable of offering. (01:46)
