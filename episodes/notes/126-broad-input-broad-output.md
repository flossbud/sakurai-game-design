# 126 · Broad Input, Broad Output

**Category:** Work Ethic · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGsxpvJ4IfM) · [Transcript](../transcripts/126-broad-input-broad-output.md)

## In one sentence
Make broad input (many games and media, taken in thoughtfully) and steady output (writing and sharing on a schedule) daily habits, because that's how a director keeps their work on par with modern games.

## Key ideas
- Directors especially need to broaden both input (stimulation from outside sources) and output (what you yourself produce), and make both a habitual part of daily life.
- His input is mostly playing other games and watching visual media. Even when busy he plays, and he tries to play as many games as possible rather than one at length, though he always tries to see the ending. He needs to grasp the scope of modern games so his own work can be on par with them; to him it's a must.
- His output is what he transmits to the world: a Weekly Famitsu column for 18 years and 9 months, a Daily Report to his dev team every day, the Screenshot of the Day on Twitter (based on the internal one), and this YouTube channel.
- "Take a lot in, put a lot out." Staying active with both, he believes, lets you achieve great things.
- People prefer different kinds of input, and you don't need to force yourself to play games. Watching something can have a big impact if you reflect on its various facets while you do. Don't let media go in one ear and out the other.

## Do this
- Play a wide range of current games instead of sinking all your time into one, and try to reach the ending so you understand the full scope of modern releases.
- While playing or watching, actively note what works and why, so the input doesn't pass straight through.
- Commit to a recurring output habit, such as a weekly column, a daily team report or a daily screenshot, and keep it going for years.
- Choose the input form that suits you (games, film, other visual media), but always take it in reflectively.

## Avoid
- Passive consumption: media that goes "in one ear and out the other."
- Narrow input from playing one title at length while missing what the rest of the field is doing.

## Examples from the episode
- **Weekly Famitsu column:** written for 18 years and 9 months, an example of habitual output.
- **Daily Report and Screenshot of the Day:** daily internal info sharing that became a public habit on Twitter.
- **This YouTube channel:** another part of his output.

## Quote
> Take a lot in, put a lot out. Stay active with both, and I think you can achieve great things. (01:34)
