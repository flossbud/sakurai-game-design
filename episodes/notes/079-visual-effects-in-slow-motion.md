# 079 · Visual Effects in Slow Motion

**Category:** Effects · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=353K_6kPqeE) · [Transcript](../transcripts/079-visual-effects-in-slow-motion.md)

## In one sentence
Effects pack a lot of information into an instant, so study them in slow motion to learn how they're built, and fill your game with plenty of exciting effects, because without them it looks plain.

## Key ideas
- Visual effects deliver a great deal of information in a single instant, which makes them hard to take in at normal speed.
- For this video he recorded many Smash Bros. Ultimate effects with slow motion on and backgrounds disabled, as a reference. Careful observation is a shortcut to success whatever you're making. The footage is also good for studying animation.
- Most of the video (about 02:00–11:00) is an unnarrated montage of slowed-down effects. The transcript only has stray on-screen text from that part.
- In play, effects fly by so fast they may not catch your eye. Remove them and the game looks plain. The same game can feel quite different with different effects.

## Do this
- Record effects (yours or a reference game's) in slow motion with the background turned off, and step through them frame by frame to see how they're built: shapes, timing, layering.
- Add debug toggles for slow motion and hiding the background, so artists can review effects in isolation.
- A/B test: turn effects off in a key scene and compare. If the plain version barely changes, your effects aren't doing enough. Add more and make them more exciting.

## Avoid
- Skimping on effects because they're only on screen for an instant.

## Examples from the episode
- **Super Smash Bros. Ultimate — effects in slow motion:** a long montage of effects with slow motion on and backgrounds disabled.
- **Super Smash Bros. Ultimate — effects removed:** the same scene without effects looks "pretty plain."

## Quote
> The same game can end up feeling quite different with different effects, so if you really want to bring your game to life, fill it with plenty of exciting effects! (11:16)
