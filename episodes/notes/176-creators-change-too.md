# 176 · Creators Change, Too

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4MCvedyOg) · [Transcript](../transcripts/176-creators-change-too.md)

## In one sentence
Creators, you included, change paths as they gain experience, so don't write off people who leave games as washed up, and give yourself room to try new fields too.

## Key ideas
- Sakurai entered the industry young, surrounded by older, experienced people and even pioneers. As he has aged, fewer and fewer of them have stayed.
- Besides people who retire, many leave the game industry partway through their careers, and for some it's unclear where they went.
- If a creator you know vanishes, don't call them "washed up" or "finished". Many have done well in other fields: some secured patents that made them a fortune, and others made groundbreaking advances in fields unrelated to games.
- Being out of the public eye often means they found fulfilling work elsewhere. As you build experience, wanting to start something new is natural, and staying in one place for a whole career is actually less common.
- There's no guarantee creators will keep doing what customers or fans want, and we shouldn't begrudge them that, because their talents may be needed more somewhere else. Everyone has the right to choose their own path and work hard in their own field.
- Sakurai is still making games, but he started this YouTube channel, something he'd never done before, so he hasn't exactly stayed put either.

## Do this
- When a creator you know leaves the industry, don't label them washed up. Assume they may be doing successful, fulfilling work somewhere you can't see.
- Don't hold it against a creator when their next project isn't what fans wanted; respect their right to choose their path.
- As your experience grows, give yourself permission to take on something new outside your usual work, as Sakurai did by starting a YouTube channel while still making games.

## Avoid
- Writing off creators who drop out of view as "washed up" or "finished".
- Expecting creators to keep doing exactly what fans want for their entire careers.
