# 099 · An Up-Close Look at Smash Bros. Stages

**Category:** Graphics · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lvYN7JciOdk) · [Transcript](../transcripts/099-an-up-close-look-at-smash-bros-stages.md)

## In one sentence
Study shipped stages up close as art reference: Smash Bros. Ultimate recreated the worlds of dozens of games in one title while holding 60 fps with eight fighters on-screen, which left the stage team little performance leeway.

## Key ideas
- Smash Bros. Ultimate has around 115 base stages ("what, 115 base stages?").
- Making them run on Nintendo Switch with eight fighters on screen at 60 frames per second "really didn't give us much leeway," so stage art had to fit a tight performance budget.
- Recreating the worlds of so many different games gives this one title "incredible breadth."
- The video is a close-up showcase of the Stage team's work, offered as reference for aspiring game artists. Almost all of it is footage with stage-name captions: Jungle Japes, Great Bay, Fountain of Dreams, Fourside, Lylat Cruise, Bridge of Eldin, Castle Siege, WarioWare Inc., Halberd, Shadow Moses Island, New Pork City, Norfair, Port Town Aero Dive, Luigi's Mansion, Gerudo Valley, Unova Pokémon League, Reset Bomb Forest, Boxing Ring, Gaur Plain, Wily Castle, Gamer, Wrecking Crew, Flat Zone X, Suzaku Castle, Midgar, Umbra Clock Tower, Moray Towers, Dracula's Castle, Mementos, Spring Stadium, Minecraft World, Mishima Dojo and Hollow Bastion.
- Making them all was hard, but Sakurai says getting a broader view of so many games is part of the fun, and the team gave it their all and had a great time.

## Do this
- When studying environment art, look at finished game stages up close to see how each recreated world is built.
- Build stage art to a performance budget that holds under the worst-case load (for Ultimate: eight fighters on screen at 60 fps).

## Examples from the episode
- **Super Smash Bros. Ultimate — stage close-ups:** 33 stages shown in detail, from Jungle Japes to Hollow Bastion, all built to run with eight fighters at 60 fps on Switch.
