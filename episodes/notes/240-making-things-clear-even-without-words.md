# 240 · Making Things Clear, Even Without Words

**Category:** UI · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=psGx8fOlus0) · [Transcript](../transcripts/240-making-things-clear-even-without-words.md)

## In one sentence
Make your game playable without reading a word: put pictures on menu icons, and make repeated presses of the confirm button lead straight into the main game.

## Key ideas
- Within limits, Sakurai tries to make his games playable even for people who don't understand the language on screen.
- He adds pictures or characters to menu icons where possible. They're more fun to look at, and he hopes they help players work out what each icon does. With the language switched to Korean as a test, the menus are hard to follow without knowing Hangul, but he feels the pictures help at least somewhat.
- For the international version of this channel, he tries to show game footage in English, because most native English speakers find Japanese very hard to read, while most Japanese people can pick up some English.
- He makes it possible to reach the main game by just pressing the confirm button over and over. In Smash Bros. Ultimate, that takes you from the title screen to Smash mode's fighter select. In Kirby Air Ride it takes you into the first stage of Air Ride mode. In Kirby Super Star it starts the first scenario, tutorial included.
- Typical interface design often produces this naturally, but he sets it up on purpose.
- Ideally a game is playable without reading a single word. He says this is rarely considered.

## Do this
- Put a picture or character on every major menu icon so its function can be guessed without text.
- Switch your game to a language you can't read and try to navigate the menus; fix any screen where you get lost.
- Set the default cursor on every screen from the title onward so that pressing confirm repeatedly starts the main mode, tutorial included if there is one.

## Examples from the episode
- **Smash Bros. Ultimate — pressing confirm repeatedly:** from the title screen straight to Smash mode's fighter select.
- **Kirby Air Ride — pressing confirm repeatedly:** into the first stage of Air Ride mode.
- **Kirby Super Star — pressing confirm repeatedly:** into the first scenario, tutorial included.
- **Menus in Korean:** even unreadable, the pictures on each icon help somewhat.
