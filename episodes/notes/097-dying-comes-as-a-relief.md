# 097 · Dying Comes as a Relief?

**Category:** Game Essence · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vDEJ0sXw8Ks) · [Transcript](../transcripts/097-dying-comes-as-a-relief.md)

## In one sentence
In competitive games, being defeated can relieve stress and defeating someone can create anxiety, so design deaths, restarts and kills so that both sides of the exchange are fun and players want another try.

## Key ideas
- A study done overseas found that players of competitive first-person shooters feel anxiety after defeating an opponent and relief when they get defeated. Being defeated releases stress, and that feeling is part of what makes games fun.
- Sakurai points to page 148 of *Think About the Video Games X* for the full analysis and summarizes three reasons:
  1. Players may not see their character as someone to empathize with. If they did, every death would make them sad, and it doesn't.
  2. The rules sweep away disappointment. Whether the clock keeps running or a rematch starts, quick restarts keep you pushing forward with no time to feel upset.
  3. Defeating an opponent may not be a real reward. Your goal is to win the match, so a KO that doesn't settle it means it's too soon to relax. If anything, it shows you a future where you're the one defeated.
- When the fun of defeating or being defeated really clicks, "there's no better entertainment." The ideal reaction to losing is "Ugh, I can't believe I lost that! But I'll get you next time!"
- How you get players to keep trying after a death deserves careful thought. Competitive games aren't only about the joy of dominating your opponent.

## Do this
- Keep respawn and rematch times short so players are back in action before disappointment sets in.
- Design the moment of being defeated to be entertaining in itself, so the loser's natural reaction is "I'll get you next time!"
- Write down, as part of the design, how you'll motivate a player to retry right after each death.

## Avoid
- Assuming competitive fun only comes from dominating opponents.
- Long gaps after a death that leave players time to dwell on the loss.

## Examples from the episode
- **Competitive first-person shooters (overseas study):** players reported anxiety after getting a kill and relief after being killed.

## Quote
> Remember, competitive games aren't only about the joy of dominating your opponent. (02:06)
