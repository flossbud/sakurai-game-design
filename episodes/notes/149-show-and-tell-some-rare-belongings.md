# 149 · Show and Tell: Some Rare Belongings

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yk8WsbCQXGc) · [Transcript](../transcripts/149-show-and-tell-some-rare-belongings.md)

## In one sentence
A tour of one-of-a-kind items from Sakurai's career. It is mostly anecdotes, but it includes a reminder that he first got through Sega's doors by submitting a game proposal to a public promotion before he worked in the industry.

## Key ideas
- Sakurai says he isn't a collector, but his position has left him with rare items that will never reach the market. He skips his trophies and awards. He shot the video alone and couldn't zoom while talking, so he cuts to close-up photos and videos instead.
- **Wii Remote with his Mii:** laser-engraved with his likeness on the back, a gift from Nintendo from around when Miis were introduced.
- **Pit and Palutena figure:** Kid Icarus: Uprising appeared as a float in the Goshogawara Tachineputa Festival. This 3D-printed figure was the reference for the float. Only about three exist, and it's so fragile he shows only a photo.
- **Autographs:** a game signed by Mr. Miyamoto ("Thanks, Mr. Sakurai!!"), and signed Kid Icarus: Uprising artwork drawn for him by manga artist Shigeyuki Fukumitsu, whose manga he had read without fail.
- **Xbox 360 Elite:** with a faceplate engraved with his name, received in December 2005 at the Xbox 360's launch from a Microsoft representative. The console died of the Red Ring of Death, and he kept using the plate on its successor.
- **Sega Associate Planner Certificate No. 004:** In the late 1980s, Sega ran a promotion where people could submit game proposals, and he won this certificate. He recalls proposing something like an action-focused Active Time Battle system, before Active Time Battle existed. Before he entered the industry, the promotion got him a visit to Sega in Otorii, Tokyo, and he later returned often for work: Mushiking, Sonic in Smash Bros., or just to play After Burner.
- Sega keeps a double-cradle After Burner cabinet in pristine condition on its ground floor, apparently preserved by measures such as 3D-printing new parts.

## Do this
- Submit your game proposals to open calls (such as a publisher's proposal promotion) even before you work in the industry. Sakurai's late-1980s entry won a certificate and got him inside Sega.
- When you record a video alone and can't operate the camera while talking, cut to close-up photos and videos to show small details.

## Examples from the episode
- **Sega proposal promotion (late 1980s):** his proposal resembled an action-focused Active Time Battle system and won Associate Planner Certificate No. 004.
- **Kid Icarus: Uprising:** a 3D-printed Pit and Palutena figure made as reference for a festival float. About three exist.
- **After Burner:** Sega's double-cradle cabinet, preserved in pristine condition through measures like 3D-printing new parts.
