# 013 · Game Awards

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re5JlUcYvxU) · [Transcript](../transcripts/013-game-awards.md)

## In one sentence
Treat awards as a celebration for creators and fans rather than a race, judge each game against its own goals and audience, and value originality, not just sales.

## Key ideas
- His view: "The game you like best is number one!" Every game has its own values, goals and audience, so he can't really get behind choosing one "best" game. What makes games great is how diverse they are, and one style isn't necessarily better than another.
- Awards are good for buzz, but their real value is a chance for creators and fans to celebrate. Creators who made something good deserve "a source of joy beyond just sales."
- That is all the more reason not to treat awards as a race: "Raising one thing up doesn't mean bringing everything around it down in the process." See them as a way to spread happiness.
- He's grateful for the awards he and his games have received. What makes them special is that "the team involved in the games was able to have their hard work immortalized in the form of an award."
- He heads the selection committee for the Game Designers Award at the Japan Game Awards. Directors of unique games cast ballots, and the prize goes to the game judged most original. He understands the criticism ("Why should game creators be a part of the judging process?") and is prepared to deal with it.
- Most Japan Game Awards are largely decided by user votes, which he calls "a popularity contest." Games that sold well naturally have more fans voting. The Game Designers Award instead puts "ingenuity—not commercial success" front and center, judged by directors "who know the struggle of creating unique games." He thinks more awards should try to bring new value to games rather than just count votes.

## Do this
- When you evaluate a game, yours or someone else's, judge it against its own goals and intended audience rather than one universal "best."
- When your game is recognized, present it as the team's achievement. Their work is what's being honored.
- If you run an award, contest or jam judging, include an originality criterion judged by people who have made unique games, alongside any popular vote.

## Avoid
- Treating awards as a race in which raising one game means tearing others down.
- Treating sales or popularity as a measure of ingenuity.

## Examples from the episode
- **Japan Game Awards — Game Designers Award:** directors of unique games vote for the most original game.
- **Japan Game Awards — other categories:** decided largely by user votes, which favors games that sold well.

## Quote
> To sum up: game awards work best as a way for creators and fans alike to celebrate great games. (03:09)
