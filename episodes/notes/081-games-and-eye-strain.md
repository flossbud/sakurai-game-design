# 081 · Games and Eye Strain

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0VaLAZeMJnE) · [Transcript](../transcripts/081-games-and-eye-strain.md)

## In one sentence
Protect your eyes for a long career. Brightness, glasses and eyedrops help, but nothing works as well as changing your work environment, for example moving your monitor farther away.

## Key ideas
- He rarely plays mobile games, and plays Switch only in TV mode, because handheld screens tire his eyes badly. He avoids playing on handhelds for any length of time.
- After a change of work setup put his monitor less than 60 cm from his eyes, his eyes tired quickly. He has always been a little farsighted, and focusing up close gets harder with age.
- After Kid Icarus: Uprising he felt his eyesight had worsened, but an optometrist measured 20/10. A recent check showed 20/15 in both eyes.
- He now keeps at least one meter between himself and the monitor. That reduces strain but requires a larger monitor, which limited his choices, but the setup is much more comfortable.
- During Smash Bros. Ultimate's development he had severe eye strain and dry eyes, which improved once it was over.
- Ways to reduce eye strain: lower screen brightness to match your surroundings, blue-light glasses, eyedrops, supplements. Still, nothing is as effective as changing your work environment.
- He admits he reads on an iPad hung above his bed, turning pages with a Bluetooth trackball, and falls asleep the moment he's sleepy.

## Do this
- Measure your eye-to-monitor distance. If it's under 60 cm, move the monitor back (he uses at least 1 m) and get a larger screen to compensate.
- Lower screen brightness to match the room you're working in.
- For long play or test sessions, prefer a TV-distance setup over handheld screens held close.
- Treat blue-light glasses, eyedrops and supplements as secondary measures, after fixing the environment.

## Avoid
- Long work sessions with a screen close to your eyes.

## Examples from the episode
- **Kid Icarus: Uprising — after development:** worried about his eyesight, he was measured at 20/10.
- **Super Smash Bros. Ultimate — development:** severe eye strain and dry eyes that eased after release.
- **Nintendo Switch:** he plays only in TV mode to avoid handheld eye strain.

## Quote
