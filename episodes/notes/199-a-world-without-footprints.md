# 199 · A World Without Footprints

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GJJT8Yoxu_U) · [Transcript](../transcripts/199-a-world-without-footprints.md)

## In one sentence
Every now and then, play a game completely blind, with no trailers, spoilers, strategy guides or Let's Plays, because it lets you feel more and it is the best way to study someone else's work.

## Key ideas
- Sakurai likes to play "as if I'm the first person walking on freshly-fallen snow." He knows nothing beforehand and sees no traces of anyone who got there first. For that reason he would rather play offline alone than online in multiplayer.
- Once he decides to experience a game or movie, he works hard to avoid spoilers. He shuts out the buzz and even closes his eyes when a trailer for it plays at the movie theater.
- He wants his impressions to be his alone, so he avoids strategy sites (a last resort when he is really stuck and wants to keep going) and Let's Plays: "If I'm going to watch a game, I might as well just try playing it."
- Spoilers and constant video uploads make this hard today, especially for story-focused works.
- He recommends going in completely blind now and again. Some people gain more from it than others, but you will at least feel more. For studying someone else's work, nothing beats it.
- He admits this is his own preference and "perhaps somewhat behind the times." It still lets him experience every surprise a work holds and keeps him in awe of the talent behind it.

## Do this
- Pick one game (or film) to experience blind. Skip its trailers, reviews, strategy guides and Let's Plays until you finish it.
- Form your own verdict first, and look up what other people think only after you are done.
- Open a guide only as a last resort, when you are truly stuck and want to keep going.
- When a game makes you curious, play it yourself instead of watching someone else play it.

## Avoid
- Letting buzz, trailers or other people's opinions shape your impressions before you have played the game yourself.

## Examples from the episode
- **Movie theater trailers:** he closes his eyes when the theater shows a trailer for something he plans to see, so he can go in knowing nothing.

## Quote
> In terms of studying someone else's work, there's no better way to do it than this. (01:44)
