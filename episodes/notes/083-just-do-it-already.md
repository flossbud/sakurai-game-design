# 083 · Just Do It Already!

**Category:** Work Ethic · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IN-Uh2VfL8c) · [Transcript](../transcripts/083-just-do-it-already.md)

## In one sentence
When you can't get started, stop thinking and start the task right now. Motivation follows action, and success comes from steady output.

## Key ideas
- Turning a 0 into a 1 is hard, and it's tough to get your engine going without fuel.
- His one solution, usable by anyone, is "just do it already." Drafting a design document? Start writing. Making art? Start drawing. Need to go out? Get dressed and go.
- He exaggerates for effect: turn off this video and get to work, as if a bomb in your room will explode unless you start now.
- When you're unmotivated there's no point overthinking it. Motivation comes later, after your hands are moving.
- Social media, video sites, the internet and even games are tempting. Cut yourself off and work.
- The best way to improve or succeed is to "just do, do, do." You won't succeed without steady output. Think about what it all means later.
- Tricks like visualizing your tasks do work, but use them after you've started.

## Do this
- When stuck, take the first physical step immediately: open the document and type the first line, or open the canvas and make the first stroke.
- Close social media, video sites and games before a work block.
- Aim for steady daily output. Save analysis and planning tricks (like task visualization) for after you've started.

## Avoid
- Waiting to feel motivated before starting.
- Overthinking a task instead of beginning it.

## Examples from the episode
- None from games. He uses everyday tasks: drafting a design document, drawing art, getting dressed to go out.

## Quote
> If you're feeling unmotivated, there's no point in overthinking it. The time is now. Get those hands moving. (01:01)
