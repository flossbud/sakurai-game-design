## Key ideas
- Unlike earlier Grab Bag topics, this one has nothing to do with games. While he posted daily Smash Bros. screens on Twitter, the thing people begged him to post more of was his cat, Fukurashi.
- She's female; her name is technically Fukura, but the family calls her Fukurashi because it rolls off the tongue better. They've had her for quite a while.
- With a cat in the house you constantly hear "She's so cute!", so the video shows photos and clips of her, including from her kitten days.
- Her high-pitched meow while rubbing against you is "simply the cutest," but she's as selfish as any cat; it's her right as queen of the household.
- Their daily routine after bathtime is giving her a Churu cat treat.
