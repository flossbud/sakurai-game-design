# 016 · Make It "Pop"

**Category:** Effects · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kcYDrtRvuKg) · [Transcript](../transcripts/016-make-it-pop.md)

## In one sentence
Don't rely on additive blending alone; mix crisp shapes and darker elements into your effects so they pop with contrast against any background.

## Key ideas
- "Effects" are elements that add visual flair, like the sparks when you hit an enemy.
- Many effects use additive blending: each element's colors are added to the background, pushing it toward white. That's different from translucency, where the colors move toward the element's own colors.
- Layering additive elements gets you "something suitable." It gives decent results from fairly simple visuals, "but that's it."
- An effect that doesn't "pop" lacks impact and contrast. It can be too bright, and it won't stand out on a light background. Keeping the whole game dark to show effects off still falls short.
- Mix in crisp effects: add polygons or simple images to the effect and make them the focus. Smash Ultimate does this with all of its effects for "a clean, anime-style look."
- Effects often look too bright in a scene, so mix in darker elements to make them visible against light backgrounds. He shows effects that originally had only bright elements, before and after.
- In any design, not just effects, opposing elements help an image stand out, "Like adding straight lines to a curvy design."

## Do this
- Check every effect against your lightest backgrounds, and add darker elements to any that wash out.
- Build each effect around crisp, solid shapes (polygons or simple images), with additive glow as support rather than the whole effect.
- Solve visibility in the effect itself instead of darkening the whole game.
- Pair opposites in any design: straight lines with curves, darks with brights.

## Avoid
- Letting additive blending do all the work.
- Effects made only of bright elements.
- Keeping the whole game dark just so effects show up.

## Examples from the episode
- **Super Smash Bros. Ultimate — all effects:** polygons or simple images are mixed in and made the focus for a clean, anime-style look.
- **Before/after effect samples:** effects with only bright elements, then with darker elements added for contrast.

## Quote
> Don't let additive blending do all the work for you! (00:48)
