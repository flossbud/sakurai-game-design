# 035 · Let Your Characters Shine

**Category:** Effects · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ucvynuIe-o) · [Transcript](../transcripts/035-let-your-characters-shine.md)

## In one sentence
Shape effects so the character stays visible inside them, through gaps, effect geometry that varies by viewing side, and outlines, rather than letting a big effect bury the character in 3D.

## Key ideas
- Effects are needed for impact, but characters often get lost in a sea of them.
- In 2D it's easy: give characters higher display priority than effects and it looks fine.
- In 3D, especially views that aren't overhead, forcing characters to draw above effects usually creates depth disparity and doesn't look good, so Smash Bros. developed workarounds.
- Terry's Power Dunk keeps him clear even inside the crown effect, and the Falcon Punch effect, which would normally hide Captain Falcon, keeps him partially visible.
- In Chrom's Up Special, the inner blue peaks are a different height seen from the front than from the back, so they don't block the character in the middle. Sakurai calls it a handy trick even in 3D games.
- The aura shown when a Final Smash is ready doesn't bury the character either. Adding an outline around the model is another way to make characters stand out.
- He admits some of this is hard to grasp without hands-on work in the tools.

## Do this
- Review every large effect while the character is inside it, at gameplay camera angles, and confirm the character stays at least partly visible.
- Shape effects with room around the character's body instead of covering it completely.
- For effects that surround a character, vary the height of the effect's parts depending on whether they're seen from the front or the back, so the parts between the camera and the character don't block it.
- In 2D, draw characters above effects; in 3D, avoid forcing character draw priority, which causes depth mismatches.
- Add an outline around character models to separate them from busy effects.

## Avoid
- Forcing 3D characters to render in front of effects, which creates depth disparity.
- Effects that completely bury the character.

## Examples from the episode
- **Super Smash Bros. — Terry's Power Dunk:** Terry stays clear even inside the crown effect.
- **Super Smash Bros. — Captain Falcon's Falcon Punch:** an effect that would normally obscure him keeps him partially visible.
- **Super Smash Bros. — Chrom's Up Special:** the inner blue peaks differ in height from front and back so they don't obstruct him.
- **Super Smash Bros. — Final Smash aura:** the character stays visible inside it.

## Quote
> Though it's tough to avoid if you want to leave an impact, sometimes characters can get lost in a sea of special effects. (00:04)
