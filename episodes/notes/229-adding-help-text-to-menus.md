# 229 · Adding Help Text to Menus

**Category:** UI · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWIbb1l7Wlw) · [Transcript](../transcripts/229-adding-help-text-to-menus.md)

## In one sentence
Add help text to menu items, write it in different words than the item's name, and trim it until its meaning is clear at a glance.

## Key ideas
- Menu items are usually one or two words, which often don't make clear what the item does. Help text, often shown at the bottom of the screen, explains it.
- Sakurai is proactive about help text and has used it in almost all his games, except those made before it was possible. He considers it a relatively vital feature.
- His must-follow rule: never explain an option using its own name. A description of "Options" that says "Go to the Options" misses the point, because the text exists for people who don't know what "Options" means. He says this happens quite often.
- Use different words, e.g., "Adjust a variety of game settings." Clarity is hard in a few words, so drill down to the most important part, cut extra words and keep polishing. Think of it as translating meaning rather than translating words.
- Mode names follow the same rule: ask whether "Play Solo" gets the point across better than "1P Mode".
- Avoid technical terms. Not everyone knows what "config" or "key repeat" means.

## Do this
- Give every menu item a one-line help text that appears when it's highlighted, e.g., at the bottom of the screen.
- Write each help line without reusing the item's name: "Options" becomes "Adjust a variety of game settings."
- Edit each line down: cut excess words, keep only the key information, and reread it as someone who doesn't know the term.
- Name modes by what the player does ("Play Solo") rather than by jargon ("1P Mode").
- Replace technical words like "config" or "key repeat" with plain descriptions.

## Avoid
- Help text that just repeats the item's name ("Go to the Options").
- Technical terms players may not know.

## Examples from the episode
- **A generic "Options" item:** "Go to the Options" fails; "Adjust a variety of game settings" works.
- **Mode naming:** "Play Solo" versus "1P Mode".

## Quote
> This is important, so I'll say it again: don't explain using the same words! (00:54)
