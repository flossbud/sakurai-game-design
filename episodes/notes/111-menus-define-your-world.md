# 111 · Menus Define Your World

**Category:** UI · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pivfg2DrFfY) · [Transcript](../transcripts/111-menus-define-your-world.md)

## In one sentence
Use fonts, UI frames, icons, colors and decoration to establish your game's world and target audience while keeping text legible, because the same content can feel adult, cute, horror or historical just by changing its presentation.

## Key ideas
- Two games with the same game essence and content can target different audiences just by changing the UI or font.
- The same options screen can look slick and adult, cute, horror-themed, bright and flashy, digital, or "Japanese," "Victorian," "Chinese" or "South Asian" in style.
- Changing the font is a much easier way to convey a world's feel than designing characters or environments, though sometimes you have to make the font from scratch, so it isn't always easy.
- Backgrounds, frames and icons count too, and together they can give your world a lot of atmosphere.
- Keep things legible while actively designing immersive visuals. Fonts and UI are part of worldbuilding, "the package that the world is wrapped in." Colors and decorations matter as well.
- Modern taste leans minimalist, but still give your fonts careful thought.

## Do this
- Mock up one representative screen (e.g., options) in several styles (slick, cute, horror, flashy, digital, regional or historical) and pick the one that best fits your world and target audience.
- Choose a font that carries the setting, and budget time to make a custom font if nothing existing fits.
- Theme the menu backgrounds, frames, icons, colors and decorations to match the world, not just the typeface.
- Check every themed font and frame for legibility at real screen size before locking it.
- Even in a minimalist UI, choose the font on purpose instead of leaving the default.

## Avoid
- Treating menus and fonts as neutral afterthoughts separate from worldbuilding.
- Styling text so heavily that it stops being legible.

## Examples from the episode
- **One options screen, many styles:** slick, cute, horror, flashy, digital, Japanese, Victorian, Chinese and South Asian versions of the same menu show how much the font and frame change the feel.

## Quote
> Fonts and UI are both a part of a game's worldbuilding. You might even say they're the package that the world is wrapped in. (01:23)
