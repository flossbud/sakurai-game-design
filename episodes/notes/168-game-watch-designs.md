# 168 · Game & Watch Designs

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8JLA5YG16QM) · [Transcript](../transcripts/168-game-watch-designs.md)

## In one sentence
When you adapt a classic character, work from the original production material (for Mr. Game & Watch, the actual LCD patterns Nintendo provided), because the original designers' ingenuity is worth studying firsthand.

## Key ideas
- Game & Watch is a series of handheld LCD games that Nintendo released starting in 1980.
- During Smash Bros. development, the team was given "truly precious" Game & Watch LCD patterns as reference.
- With special permission from Nintendo, Sakurai shows some of those patterns. The episode is almost entirely a silent visual tour, with narration only at the start and end (about 72 words of subtitles across roughly four minutes).
- His only comment on them: it's fun just to look at them, and they're "a true testament to the unique ingenuity of the era."

## Do this
- When adapting a character from an older game, get the original production material (sprites, LCD patterns, model sheets) and use it as your reference rather than memory or screenshots.
- Study art made under tight hardware limits, such as fixed LCD segments, as examples of how much ingenuity a constraint can draw out.

## Examples from the episode
- **Game & Watch / Super Smash Bros. — LCD patterns as reference:** Nintendo supplied the original LCD patterns to the Smash team during development, and some are shown in this episode with special permission.

## Quote
> They're a true testament to the unique ingenuity of the era. (04:08)
