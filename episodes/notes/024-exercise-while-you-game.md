# 024 · Exercise While You Game!

**Category:** Grab Bag · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ysaNUatLMn0) · [Transcript](../transcripts/024-exercise-while-you-game.md)

## In one sentence
Protect your health and productivity by folding light, sustained exercise into time you already spend playing, and don't overdo it.

## Key ideas
- Games eat up a lot of time, so Sakurai makes up for it by playing on an exercise bike. Pedaling while playing takes his mind off the effort, so sessions that would normally be tough breeze by; he has pedaled for two whole hours.
- Aerobic exercise works best when it's sustained over a period of time, and playing a game makes you forget how tiring it is. A stepper or treadmill works too if your living situation allows.
- The game changes how you exercise: in a stealth game you may unconsciously pedal slower, while a racing game can be great exercise if you think of pedaling as a way to go faster.
- He often watches a video or TV show on a separate TV at the same time. With this much multitasking one thing may not sink in, so he pauses one of them when he really wants to concentrate. With so much subscription content around, he feels watching this way beats not watching at all.
- He admits he really doesn't like exercising, but people who exercise tend to be far more productive than people who don't, so it's better to exercise at least a little.
- After COVID-19 stopped his gym visits he exercised at home with adjustable dumbbells and by climbing his apartment building's stairs (around 500 steps). He went overboard and hurt his legs, which is his reason for the closing advice: don't overdo it.

## Do this
- Put an exercise bike (or a stepper or treadmill) in front of the screen and do your play sessions on it, aiming for long, steady aerobic sessions rather than short bursts.
- Choose games that suit pedaling, such as racing games where you can imagine pedaling makes you go faster; slow, careful games like stealth games tend to make you pedal less.
- When you multitask (playing while a show runs on a second screen), pause one of them whenever you need to concentrate on the other.
- Do at least a little regular exercise at home, such as adjustable dumbbells, even if you dislike exercise, because it keeps you productive.
- Ramp up gradually and take a break if something starts to hurt.

## Avoid
- Overdoing it: he hurt his legs climbing around 500 stairs and had to stop.

## Examples from the episode
