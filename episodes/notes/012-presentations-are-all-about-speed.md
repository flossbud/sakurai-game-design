# 012 · Presentations Are All About Speed!

**Category:** Work Ethic · [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkNSNGJvFXk) · [Transcript](../transcripts/012-presentations-are-all-about-speed.md)

## In one sentence
Keep idea presentations fast-moving: cut the fat, go straight to the heart of the topic, drop justifications and alternatives, and use simple graphics.

## Key ideas
- If you're on a creative team you'll have to present your ideas, and even solo creators need to communicate them to others.
- His secret: "Presentations are all about speed!"
- Trim the fat and get right to the heart of the topic. "Forget justifications or alternatives."
- Use graphics, but keep them simple.
- Speed doesn't mean short. "It doesn't have to be short. Just keep things moving and make the most of the time you have."
- The episode itself, about a minute long, demonstrates the idea: "Quick, snappy, and memorable."

## Do this
- Cut every slide or section that doesn't directly serve your core idea, and open with the idea itself.
- Leave justifications and alternative options out of the main pitch.
- Replace dense text with simple graphics.
- Rehearse for pace, not brevity. Use the time you have, but never let it stall.

## Avoid
- Long preambles and defending your choices before you've shown them.
- Laying out several alternatives instead of the idea you're proposing.
- Busy, complicated graphics.
