[00:23](https://youtu.be/b3Fd8pBK0EA?t=23) In Japan, we used to have a card-based arcade game called Kouchuu Ouja Mushiking. It released in 2003. When you put in 100 yen, the machine would print out a card with a rhino or stag beetle on it, and by scanning that with the barcode reader, you could make the Mushi* on the cards battle each other. * Insects These battles took place via rock-paper-scissors.

[00:44](https://youtu.be/b3Fd8pBK0EA?t=44) Each Mushi specialized in one of the three shapes, which led to the game's push and pull. Do you play your strong paper, or try to win through surprise? It's all about reading your opponent.

[00:55](https://youtu.be/b3Fd8pBK0EA?t=55) This may be difficult to picture for younger and overseas viewers, but Mushiking was a pretty big hit, especially with early elementary-schoolers.
