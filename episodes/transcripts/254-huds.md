[00:04](https://youtu.be/a_HB81ujHHE?t=4) Information overlaid on the play area of the game screen is known as a "HUD." It's short for "heads-up display." Sometimes it's called other things as well, such as "onscreen info."

[00:18](https://youtu.be/a_HB81ujHHE?t=18) HUDs are actually displayed in places such as fighter planes using a half-mirror— a feature that's also recently become popular in cars, giving drivers an array of fun features. You don't have to look away from the road, and the display appears as if at a distance, making it a safer way to get information.

[00:37](https://youtu.be/a_HB81ujHHE?t=37) A game HUD includes all of the elements that get placed at the forefront of the game screen. On a screen like this, it would be these parts.
