[00:47](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=47) In the case of Smash Bros., up through Smash Bros. Melee, I made the call on how parameters should be adjusted. By "parameters," I don't mean just strength, but things like mobility and everything else, too. I'd play against other team members, then determine who felt too strong, too weak, or not unique enough, and make adjustments myself.

[01:07](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=67) The development time was short, and on top of my game director duties, I had to set all those parameters as well, so it took all I had just to finish. Looking back now, it's sometimes hard to believe the games ever got done.

[01:22](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=82) To be precise, I kept doing things like this up through Kirby Air Ride. Then, starting with Smash Bros. Brawl, we added play testers to the team. Though, there were only four of them. And they weren't successful tournament players or anything.

[01:38](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=98) It was exactly the number we needed for a Brawl mode match (four). I wouldn't say they were the most skilled players, either. Plus, since online updates weren't possible until after Smash Bros. Brawl, the game balance had to stay the same as it was at release, and we couldn't even gather match data.

[01:57](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=117) After Kid Icarus: Uprising and Smash Bros. for 3DS/Wii U, Smash Bros. Ultimate saw a huge boost to our play testing team. We brought in more testers, and greater talent, too. Furthermore, online matches and tournaments had grown rather popular, making it easy for us to use their results as reference.

[02:16](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=136) I think it'd be hard to get things much more balanced than this. My personal approach to character tuning is to make each fighter highly unique. Having players with divided opinions is ideal, as that's part of the fun. Looking at any one part in isolation may make the game seem unfair, but I'm willing to write that off for the sake of individuality.

[02:39](https://youtu.be/TayNJ4603Hc?t=159) I'm glad that top tournament players use a wide range of fighters, but that's not the only thing we look at.
