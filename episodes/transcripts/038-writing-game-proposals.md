[00:14](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=14) I'd like to talk to the extent I can about the format I’ve used for game proposals in recent years. Personally, I find this approach works best, but I understand if folks from other dev teams or companies find it a bit unusual. This is the cover page for my Smash Bros. Ultimate proposal.

[00:33](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=33) It all started here. I made the proposal in PowerPoint, and almost all the pages look like this. One screenshot or diagram, and just two lines of text. Each page is its own topic that works within these limits.

[00:53](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=53) Though...there are lots of pages. Over 200 of them, in fact. Still, I make them quick and snappy, like panels in a comic— so it doesn't take all that long to get through the presentation.

[01:07](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=67) Plus, some of it goes deep on mechanics required for in-game implementation— in other words, content that only the dev team really needs to know. You'll often hear that it's best to keep your proposals lean, but that's not always the case.

[01:23](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=83) You just have to be clear, engaging, and to the point. As long as you move through a dense presentation quickly, page count itself isn't really an issue. And besides, it's Smash's fault for having so much content.

[01:40](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=100) Not much we can do about that! I break things down into categories and subcategories, so people can follow along and see just how far into the presentation we are. Nobody likes an endless presentation, after all.

[01:55](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=115) I started using this proposal format around the time I went freelance. I do say a little about each slide as I'm presenting, but I never read straight off the page. It can be pretty annoying when a slide is just a wall of text the presenter reads out loud.

[02:14](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=134) People just end up reading ahead. If you're guilty of this yourself, try changing it up.

[02:22](https://youtu.be/2Iiu1k16Ukc?t=142) If you want to make your proposals easy to digest, I fully recommend the method I just showed you. But don't forget: the most important thing is what's written inside.
