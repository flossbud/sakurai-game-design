[01:33](https://youtu.be/Y0sf-emY5oE?t=93) Targeting and market research data is built on everything that's come before. They don't show the path to what comes next. The boundaries between audiences are very blurry. Some older folks like cute things, while some kids prefer more intense content.

[02:13](https://youtu.be/Y0sf-emY5oE?t=133) So if I had to name a target audience, it would be "beginners." Not any specific age range. Despite being designed for beginners, the game still has some oomph to it, and even advanced players can enjoy how it controls.

[02:46](https://youtu.be/Y0sf-emY5oE?t=166) It simply tells you a safer route—the main road. This is the way most people have chosen to go. But if you keep following that path, you'll end up producing work that is increasingly more average and mediocre—and that sells less.
