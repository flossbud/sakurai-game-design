[05:16](https://youtu.be/snHecpmPeJs?t=316) One rare feature that helps Meteos stand out from other falling-block puzzle games is that each planet is unique in terms of elements like playing fields and blocks. In most falling-block puzzle games, even if you're able to pick a character, the gameplay stays the same, while only the moves or skills available to you are slightly different.

[05:37](https://youtu.be/snHecpmPeJs?t=337) In Meteos, however, every planet is distinct— not just with the aliens or background art, but the block shapes, playing field width, launch characteristics, music, sound effects, and more.
