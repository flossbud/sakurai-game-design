[00:03](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=3) Say you need to decide on something, so you gather your team together for a meeting— but you're unable to reach a consensus. At this point, many managers would say, "I'll have to decide on this later!" To this I'd say: please don't do that!

[00:23](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=23) Generally speaking, decisions should be made on the spot. That may not be possible without the responsible parties present, but assuming there's someone there with the authority to decide, please push them to do so. And if you're the person with that authority, be sure to make your decision quickly.

[00:39](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=39) Don't put off making those calls! Make a decision, and do it now! As the name implies, a director decides the direction of nearly every aspect of a game's development. Each decision represents a fork in the road that will send your team down a long production path, so one wrong choice could lead to many hours of wasted work.

[01:02](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=62) That's a truly massive responsibility. Thus, I can understand the desire to be careful, get second opinions, and figure things out as you go, but there often isn't time for that kind of hesitation. A quick, firm decision can end up helping more of your team than you might imagine.

[01:21](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=81) Every minute they have to wait is a minute the issue remains unresolved. The rest of the team should also be working to facilitate the decision. After all, the person deciding needs accurate information. If the relevant resources aren't reliable, the decision itself won't be, either.

[01:37](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=97) That said, while it's important to not put decisions off for later, it's simultaneously important to remain flexible. If you notice a mistake somewhere, don't just stay the course because the decision has already been made— leave some leeway to correct the error.

[01:55](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=115) Since decisions set the trajectory of your project, frequent slight adjustments can ensure that things stay on the right path.

[02:06](https://youtu.be/NaWuNgpYDi4?t=126) Personally speaking, I try to make a call as soon as a team member comes to me. If I need more information, I'll do the necessary research, too. When it comes to making decisions, it's best to think that "later" is too late!
