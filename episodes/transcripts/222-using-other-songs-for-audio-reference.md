But there's some danger in doing so, too. For one thing, when a director gets too fixated on a particular piece, they may end up closing their mind to other possibilities.

[00:41](https://youtu.be/heFZ6qQXfDw?t=41) Everything will sound like a cheap imitation— and you can't lock yourself down like that. On the other hand, the composer themselves may get too fixated. Simply put, they might have a hard time trying to make something similar, yet somehow different. They might also be unsure of what parts of the reference to keep, and what parts to change— a surprisingly common way to lose sight of your goal, I'd say.
