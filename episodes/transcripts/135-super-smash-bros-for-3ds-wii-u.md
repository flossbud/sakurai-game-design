[01:30](https://youtu.be/DeLrIXCOUw0?t=90) Only “one" person can play on a handheld, while a home console allows for “many." Was there a way to link their merits? [03:56](https://youtu.be/DeLrIXCOUw0?t=236) A feature of 3DS multiplayer is that each player has their own screen to play on, and this mode takes full advantage of that. Players can explore a huge map however they want, a feature which wasn't possible in previous Smash Bros. titles.
