[00:04](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=4) For directors especially, if you want to be able to do your best work, I feel it's essential that you broaden both your input and your output.

[00:15](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=15) Input is stimulation you get from outside sources. Output is what you yourself produce. I recommend you make both a habitual part of your daily life. In my case, a large part of my input is playing other games and consuming visual media.

[00:33](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=33) I play a lot of different games. Sometimes  struggle to find time for them given how busy I am, but I do at least play them. And rather than play one title at length, I try to play as many games as I can.

[00:49](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=49) Though, I always try to see the ending. I need to grasp the scope of modern games so that what I deliver can be on par with that. To me, it's a must. My output would be the things I transmit into the world, so, to list some examples...

[01:08](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=68) First, I wrote a column in Weekly Famitsu magazine for 18 years and 9 months. I think you could call that habitual. On the game dev front, I wrote a Daily Report to share info and talk about anything that warranted discussion.

[01:22](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=82) I did it every day. The Screenshot of the Day that I uploaded on Twitter was based on a similar thing I did internally with my dev team. That's another way of making output a habit.

[01:34](https://youtu.be/VGsxpvJ4IfM?t=94) I'd say this YouTube channel is also a part of my output. Take a lot in, put a lot out. Stay active with both, and I think you can achieve great things.
