Drifting is a method of clearing a curve in which you let your wheels slide ağainst the turn. In a real car, drifting is obviously not as fast as grip driving, but if we're trying to make our game fun, a great way to do so is to make drifting itself fun.

[01:05](https://youtu.be/7VD0K1Sr5u0?t=65) Next, let's analyze it. Why does drifting feel fun? It's because the action has both risk and reward. The player loses control, with nothing but walls in front of them—a dangerous situation.

[01:19](https://youtu.be/7VD0K1Sr5u0?t=79) But if they brake well and clear the curve, they come out on the other side with an advantage. Now, how do you go about making drifting even more fun? Boosting the player's score for a drift doesn't really have any tangible benefits. If you award the player permanent power-ups, skilled drifters will run away with it too easily.

[01:42](https://youtu.be/7VD0K1Sr5u0?t=102) Boosting speed for a successful drif... Well, there's an idea. If nothing else, we need to give players a bit of speed so that their risk is rewarded. Last, we rebuild.
